Garmin MFA code

Garmin sent a one-time code to your email or phone. Enter it to finish logging in; sync then restarts.

Log in to Garmin

Sync needs your Garmin Connect login.

The password is used for this login only and never stored. wishmap keeps the resulting Garmin session encrypted with a key only your wishmap login unlocks.

Strava

Upload your Strava data export (export_….zip, or just its activities.csv) so synced Garmin activities take their Strava names and sports.

Share with other users

They can show your routes (name, sport, status, tags; never notes or ratings) on their own map. Synced activities usually start at your home.

Public link

Anyone with the link can view your map, read-only and without an account. Rotate replaces the link (the old one stops working); revoke takes it down.

API tokens

Let scripts and agents read or change your pins, routes and ratings, sending Authorization: Bearer <token>. A token never reaches your account, Garmin login or these settings.
